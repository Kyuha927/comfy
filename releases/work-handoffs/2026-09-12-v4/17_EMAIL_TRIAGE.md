# ONE-SHOT WORK HANDOFF — Email Triage / Personal Interest Filter

## Model routing
- MAIN triage owner: **GPT-5.6 Sol — Medium**
- High-volume first-pass classifier: **GPT-5.6 Luna — Medium**
- Ambiguity/importance verifier: **GPT-5.6 Terra — Medium**
- Astra is not used by default; use **GPT-6 Astra — High** only for a genuinely high-stakes ambiguous decision.
- Never use Pro in Work.

Take ownership of email triage with the goal of aggressively reducing attention cost. Most email should not reach the final report.

Use available Gmail history and current mail. Starred/preserved mail is preference evidence but distinguish important/actionable mail from material starred merely for archive/reference.

Surface only messages requiring action/decision, genuinely important personal/account/service changes, valuable opportunities, or high-value AI/model/tool/product developments that match demonstrated interests. Suppress low-value newsletters, routine notifications, duplicate promotions, and noise.

Luna performs broad first-pass classification. Terra checks borderline importance and deadlines. Sol makes the final user-facing selection and summary. Do not delete, archive, label, or mutate mail unless separately authorized.

For surfaced mail provide concise content, why it matters, required action, and deadline. Keep the output short enough to read.
