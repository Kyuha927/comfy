# ONE-SHOT WORK HANDOFF — AI Music Video Production

## Model routing
- MAIN creative/system owner: **GPT-6 Astra — XHigh**
- Shot/temporal/character-continuity QA: **GPT-6 Astra — XHigh**
- Pipeline/API/edit/upscale implementation: **GPT-5.6 Sol — High**
- Repeatable clip and failure tests: **GPT-5.6 Terra — High**
- Provider/pricing/limit research: **GPT-5.6 Luna — Medium**
- Never use Pro in Work.

Take ownership of the user's end-to-end AI music-video workflow across relevant current systems such as Seedance, MiniMax/Hailuo, Veo, Runway, Grok video, image-to-video, anime/2.5D pipelines, temporal enhancement, and editing.

Optimize the complete chain: concept → storyboard → reference lock → shot generation → continuity → performance/lip sync when relevant → temporal stability → upscale/restoration → edit → final QC. Do not optimize one isolated clip at the expense of the finished video.

Determine with evidence when native high-resolution generation wins, when lower-resolution + temporal enhancement wins, when frame-by-frame upscaling introduces flicker, and where reference/character locking breaks.

Astra owns visual and temporal judgment. Sol builds/repairs the production pipeline. Terra executes repeatable comparisons. Luna gathers current provider constraints but does not choose the winner.

Where tools permit, generate or inspect representative tests. End with one canonical production workflow plus quality/cost-ranked fallbacks and explicit evidence.
